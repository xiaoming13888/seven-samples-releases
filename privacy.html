Privacy Policy — SEVEN SAMPLES

Effective date: 27 November 2026. Developer: Evercrest. Contact: xiaomingfacaiba@gmail.com

What this app collects

SEVEN SAMPLES does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and does not access your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (such as your biomass bank, settings, and local crash diagnostics) is stored only on your own device and never leaves it.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (supply packs and a one-time gold suit unlock) are processed entirely by Google Play. Payment information is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy. The app itself only receives a purchase confirmation (which product was bought) so it can grant the item in-game.

Network

The Google Play version of the app makes no network requests of its own. Updates are delivered through Google Play.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
